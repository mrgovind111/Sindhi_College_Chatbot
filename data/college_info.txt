ABOUT SINDHI COLLEGE
--------------------
Sindhi College, Bengaluru was established in 2002 under the Sindhi Seva Samiti,
a philanthropic organisation. It was inaugurated by Sri L.K. Advani (then Deputy
Prime Minister of India) and Sri S.M. Krishna (then Chief Minister of Karnataka).
The college has 1300+ students, 60+ faculty members, and 40+ recruiters.

Accreditation: NAAC Accredited with B++ Grade
Affiliation: Permanently affiliated to Bangalore University;
Recognized by UGC under section 2(f) & 12(B)

ADMISSION DETAILS
-----------------
Eligibility: Passed 10+2 / II PUC or equivalent from a recognized board.

Documents Required:
- Scanned 10th Marks card
- Scanned 12th / II PUC result copy
- Scanned Aadhaar card
- Scanned passport-size photo

Admission Process: Apply online through the college website or offline on campus.
A minimum payment of Rs. 10,000/- is required to block the seat.
